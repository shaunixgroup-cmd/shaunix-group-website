FAQ

Questions schools ask us.

Is the certificate government recognised?

No. SHAUNIX SKILL BUILDERS is a private skill-development program. Students receive a Certificate of Participation from SHAUNIX SKILL BUILDERS, which is not a government-recognised qualification.

Who brings the materials and kits?

We bring the kits and tools. What is included for each batch is written clearly in the school agreement, so there are no hidden charges for parents.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. We use low-voltage kits only, assemble PCs with the power off, keep sessions supervised, and share trainer ID details with the school.

When can the program run?

After school hours, during activity periods or in vacations, whichever suits your timetable.

Can we choose the track?

Yes. Choose from AI, Coding, PC Hardware, Electronics, Green Energy or Cyber Safety. We recommend the right level for each class group.

SHAUNIX SKILL BUILDERS is a vertical of SHAUNIX GROUP. Participation certificates do not carry any government or board recognition.
