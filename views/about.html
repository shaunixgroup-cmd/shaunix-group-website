Who Is SHAUNIX GROUP?

Born from service. Grown by trust.

SHAUNIX GROUP started where most good businesses do — fixing problems for real people.

Laptops were repaired honestly. Genuine parts replaced sketchy ones. Customers kept returning with bigger needs: a website, printed brochures, a course for their child, even safe disposal of old office computers.

Instead of sending them elsewhere, we built dedicated specialist teams for each need — and that is how six verticals grew naturally around one core belief: do it right, or don't do it.

  • Sold — genuine IT products & accessories
  • Serviced — expert repairs & upgrades
  • Created — websites, apps & branding
  • Learned — future-ready skills taught
  • Printed — complete printing solutions
  • Recovered — IT assets reused responsibly
